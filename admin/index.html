    How to decide

    Judge the content, not the relationship. Most reports are people who know each other disagreeing. Zzzap doesn't referee that: Hide, Mute, Block and Remove Follower do, and a dismissed report tells the reporter how.

    Remove only if it crosses a line

    • Threats: harm to a person or their things, encouraging self-harm. Knowing real details (where they live or park) makes it a threat even as a "joke".
    • Hate: attacking someone for who they are.
    • Sexual content, or anything sexual involving a minor.
    • Sharing someone's private info without consent.
    • Spam, scams, dangerous links. Illegal activity. Impersonation.
    • Harassment: a pattern aimed at one person, not one rude line.

    Everything else: Dismiss

    Rude, sarcastic, blunt, a bad take, strong words between friends. "The line is targeting people, not seasoning language."

    Same steps for everyone

    • First time over a line: Warn.
    • Second time: Warn (final).
    • Third time: Suspend.
    • Real threats, anything sexual involving a minor, sharing private info: Suspend right away.

    Reports can be abuse too

    Someone whose reports keep getting dismissed, especially about the same person, is marked "Reports a lot". Lean toward Dismiss.

    Claude's suggestion

    Each new report gets a suggested button and one reason, from these same rules. It helps you think; you decide.
